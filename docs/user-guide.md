# User guide

---

## Setup

1. Plug the system into mains power (230 V) and wait for the touchscreen to start up. The gantry homes automatically.
2. Fill the source beaker or tubes. You or a technician can do this.
3. Place the labware in the marked slots on the base. The grooves guide each item into position.
4. Fit a fresh pipette tip.

---

## Running a job

1. **Choose source:** tap Beaker or Tubes. Reset clears all your selections.
2. **Pick the source:**
   - Beaker: tap Left or Right.
   - Tubes: tap one well on the grid, then Confirm.
3. **Output wells:** tap one or more wells, then Confirm. Clear resets the grid.
4. **Volume:** enter the volume in µL and tap Start Pipetting.

A two-tone beep confirms the job. A short melody plays when the head has returned home.

Back takes you to the previous screen, and Home returns to the start. The system warns you and blocks the job if you select no input well, no output wells, the same well as both input and output, or an out-of-range volume.

---

## After use

- Remove the tips by hand. There is no auto-eject yet. Dispose of them according to your lab's waste protocol.
- Keep liquids away from the exposed electronics.
